### Accounts and preferences

Authentication mechanisms recognise your signed-in session. Fabledose also stores the selected child profile in localStorage, separately for each account. Reading history, favourites and account settings are also kept in the database, not solely in cookies.

Your browser may retain a NEXT_LOCALE language preference. Clearing site data may sign you out or reset local preferences; it does not delete your account or database records.

### Analytics — the current implementation needs correction

The browser code initialises Google Analytics through Firebase. Actual identifiers and data collection depend on configuration and need checking in production.

The analytics and marketing switches save account preferences but currently do not control Analytics initialisation. They should not be treated as working controls blocking all optional technologies. Before publication, optional analytics must actually remain blocked until any required consent is obtained.

### Marketing and external websites

Free is intended to be sponsored. This does not mean a particular advertising network is already running or that its technologies have been identified. We do not invent advertising cookie names or lifetimes.

Substack and social media links lead to external websites governed by their operators’ policies. Any future embedded external tools will require a separate assessment and an update to this description.

### How should choices work?

The planned “Cookie settings” link will open a panel available without signing in. It will allow optional categories to be accepted or rejected and choices to be changed later. Refusing optional analytics or marketing should not in itself disable the basic service.

This describes planned behaviour, not a completed feature. Strictly necessary storage must be distinguished from optional technologies and its purpose justified. Saving a choice must actually control which tools run.

### What can you do in your browser?

Browser settings allow you to clear site data or restrict storage. This may affect sign-in and saved preferences. Clearing browser storage does not undo data already sent to an analytics provider or replace an account erasure request.

### What needs completing before publication?

We will confirm the names, providers, purposes and lifetimes of cookies and other identifiers. Network requests must be checked before consent, after refusal, after acceptance and after withdrawal, including while signed out. Only then will we publish an inventory matching the actual service.
